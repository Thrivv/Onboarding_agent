1. Who can open a corporate bank account in the UAE?
Any registered business in the UAE with a valid trade license — including mainland, free zone, or offshore entities — can apply.
2. Can a sole proprietorship open a corporate bank account?
Yes, sole proprietors can open a business account in their trade name with the appropriate license and documents.
3. Can I open a corporate account for a free zone company?
Yes, free zone companies are eligible. We require the trade license and documents issued by the free zone authority.
4. Can offshore companies open accounts?
Some offshore jurisdictions are accepted, subject to additional due diligence. Please check with our onboarding team.
5. Is physical presence required for opening a corporate account?
Yes, all shareholders and authorized signatories are typically required for in-person verification or video KYC (if available).
6. What additional documents are needed for a multi-owner company?
You must provide identification and signatures of all shareholders. A Board Resolution outlining signing authority is required.x
7. Is a tenancy contract or office space required?
Yes, a valid office lease agreement or Ejari is required as part of the compliance checks.
8. Do you require a VAT certificate to open a business account?
Not necessarily. If you're registered for VAT, submission of the TRN certificate is mandatory. If not, a declaration may be required.
9. Do I need audited financials or bank statements?
For new businesses, a business plan and source of funds are sufficient. Established businesses may need financial statements and previous bank records.
10. How long does it take to open a corporate account?
The standard time frame is 7 to 15 working days, depending on the business activity, risk level, and documentation.
11. What is the minimum balance requirement?
It varies by account type but typically starts at AED 25,000 to AED 100,000. Falling below this may incur monthly fees.
12. Are there account opening or maintenance fees?
Yes. These vary depending on the account package and balance requirements. A schedule of fees is provided during onboarding.
13. Can I apply for multiple currencies in my corporate account?
Yes, we offer multi-currency corporate accounts for eligible businesses.
14. Can I open more than one business account for the same company?
Yes, multiple accounts can be opened for different purposes (e.g., payroll, operations), subject to internal approval.
15. Can I assign multiple signatories to the account?
Yes. You can define one or more authorized signatories. Their details must be included in the Board Resolution and supported by valid IDs.
16. Can I change signatories later?
Yes, changes can be made by submitting an updated Board Resolution and updated documentation.
17. What is the difference between a shareholder and a signatory?
A shareholder owns a portion of the company; a signatory is authorized to operate the account. They can be the same or different individuals.
18. Can I open a corporate account for a newly registered company with no transactions yet?
Yes, we accept newly formed companies. A business plan, initial capital deposit, and background of the owners will be required.
19. Do you onboard businesses in high-risk sectors (e.g., crypto, finance, consultancy)?
Some sectors are subject to enhanced due diligence or restrictions. Please consult our compliance team during the application.
21. Can I open a corporate bank account in the UAE if I’m not a resident?
Yes, non-residents can open a corporate account in the UAE, but the process involves stricter due diligence and longer processing times. You will need:
      * A valid trade license (mainland or free zone)
      * Passport copies of all shareholders
      * Company incorporation documents (MOA/AOA, share certificates)
      * Proof of address for shareholders (e.g., utility bill from home country)
      * Business plan, source of funds, and anticipated turnover
      * Board resolution appointing an authorized signatory (who may need to be a UAE resident)
22. What is a board resolution, and why is it needed?
      * A board resolution is an official document where the company's board of directors authorizes specific individuals to open and operate the bank account on behalf of the company. 
23. Can I apply for credit facilities along with the corporate account?
      * Yes, businesses can apply for credit facilities such as overdrafts, loans, and credit cards. Approval depends on the company's creditworthiness and the bank's lending criteria.
24. Can I set up multiple users with different access levels for online banking?
      * Yes, corporate online banking systems typically allow the creation of multiple user profiles with customizable access rights and transaction limits.
